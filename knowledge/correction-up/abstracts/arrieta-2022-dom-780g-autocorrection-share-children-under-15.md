# Comparison of MiniMed 780G System Performance in Users Aged Younger and Older than 15 Years: Evidence from 12 870 Real-World Users

- **Authors:** Arrieta A, Battelino T, Scaramuzza AE, Da Silva J, Castañeda J, Cordero TL, Shin J, Cohen O
- **Journal/year:** Diabetes, Obesity and Metabolism 2022;24(7):1370-1379
- **DOI:** 10.1111/dom.14714
- **URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9545031/
- **Licence:** paraphrased (open access under CC BY-NC 4.0; summarised with numbers, no long verbatim text)
- **Saved:** summary from the PMC full text on 2026-10-21 for the Mellis RAG corpus.

## What it is

Real-world CareLink analysis of MiniMed 780G users split by self-reported age (<=15 vs >15 years). Shows how much correction insulin the 780G gives automatically in children and how user-initiated boluses change.

## Methods

- Uploads Aug 2020-Jul 2021; 12,870 users with >=10 days of data (3211 aged <=15 y); pre/post-AHCL subset.

## Results relevant to correction boluses

- <=15 y: TIR 73.9 %, GMI 6.8 %, time <70 mg/dL 3.2 % (adults 76.5 %, 2.3 %); 92.7 % time in auto mode.
- In children auto-corrections were 6.0 +/- 4.4 U/day = **15 % of total daily insulin and about 25 % of all bolus insulin**; adults 13.5 % of TDD.
- After AHCL start in children TDD rose 13.4 % (30.5 -> 34.6 U), mainly from auto-corrections (5.4 U/day, 25.5 % of boluses), while **user-initiated boluses fell in number and amount**.
- Settings in children (<=15 y): target 100 mg/dL + active insulin time 2 h gave the best TIR (78.9 %, GMI 6.6 %) but **time <70 mg/dL 3.6 %**; target 110 mg/dL + 2 h gave TIR 76.5 % with time <70 mg/dL 2.7 %. The authors suggest children start at 110 mg/dL + 2 h and move to 100 mg/dL if there are no concerns - i.e. a more aggressive correction setting bought ~2.4 TIR points at the cost of ~0.9 points more time below 70 in children.

## Limitations

- Manufacturer-authored (several Medtronic employees); age self-reported in bands; no event-level analysis of hypoglycaemia after user corrections; 780G auto-correction logic differs from oref SMB.
