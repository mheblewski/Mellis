# Artificial Pancreas With Carbohydrate Suggestion Performance for Unannounced and Announced Exercise in Type 1 Diabetes

- **Authors:** Viñals C, Beneyto A, Martín-SanJosé JF, Furió-Novejarque C, Bertachi A, Bondia J, Vehí J, Conget I, Giménez M
- **Journal/year:** Journal of Clinical Endocrinology & Metabolism 2021
- **DOI:** 10.1210/clinem/dgaa562
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32852548/ (NCT03577158)
- **Licence:** paraphrased
- **Saved:** summary from the PubMed/Europe PMC abstracts of this trial and of Mesa et al. 2023 (Diabetes Res Clin Pract, 10.1016/j.diabres.2023.110956) on 2026-10-21 for the Mellis RAG corpus.

## Design

- Research group of Vehí, Bondia and Beneyto. Multivariable closed-loop controller that also recommends rescue carbohydrate automatically when its model predicts hypoglycaemia.
- Randomized 3-arm crossover in 10 adult pump users (mean age 40.8 y, HbA1c 7.3%): closed loop with unannounced exercise, closed loop with announced exercise, open-loop pump therapy. Heavy cycling session (3 × 15 min).

## Results

- Time < 70 mg/dL during and 3 h after exercise: median 0.0% in both closed-loop arms vs 16.2% open loop (p = 0.047 and 0.063).
- Time 70–180 mg/dL: 87.8% and 91.9% vs 81.1%.
- Achieved with similar insulin and LESS total carbohydrate than open loop.

## Follow-up: Mesa et al. 2023

- Same system (hybrid closed loop + automatic carbohydrate suggestion) in 11 hypo-prone adults over 32 h in hospital (night, 4 meals, 2 unannounced exercise sessions): time < 70 mg/dL median 0.0%; median 4 suggestions of 15 g carbohydrate per person.

## Notes for Mellis

- **Relevance:** Prediction-triggered rescue carbs in fixed 15 g portions, driven by a glucose forecast, prevented lows with fewer total carbs than reactive treatment. The exact recommendation rule (how the amount and trigger are computed) is in the engineering papers (Beneyto et al., IEEE) which were NOT read here — not verified.
- **Population:** adults, pumps, research AID; exercise context, not meals in children.
- **Evidence level:** small randomized crossover (n = 10) and pilot (n = 11).
