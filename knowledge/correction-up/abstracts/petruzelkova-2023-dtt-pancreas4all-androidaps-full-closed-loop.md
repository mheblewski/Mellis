# First Use of Open-Source Automated Insulin Delivery AndroidAPS in Full Closed-Loop Scenario: Pancreas4ALL Randomized Pilot Study

- **Authors:** Petruzelkova L, Neuman V, Plachy L, Kozak M, Obermannova B, Kolouskova S, Pruhova S, Sumnik Z
- **Journal/year:** Diabetes Technology & Therapeutics 2023;25(5):315-323
- **DOI:** 10.1089/dia.2022.0562
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36826996/
- **Licence:** paraphrased (abstract only)
- **Saved:** summary from the PubMed/Europe PMC abstract on 2026-10-21 for the Mellis RAG corpus.

## What it is

Randomised crossover camp study (Prague) of oref-based AndroidAPS in adolescents: full meal boluses vs meal announcement only vs no announcement (full closed loop relying on SMB/UAM).

## Methods

- 16 adolescents/young adults (15-20 y, mean 17 y), HbA1c 56 mmol/mol; three 3-day camp periods; locked AndroidAPS 3.1.03 ("Pancreas4ALL"); order of announcement-only and full-closed-loop periods randomised.

## Results relevant to correction boluses

- System in control 95 % of time; time <3.0 mmol/L (<54 mg/dL) 0.72 % overall.
- **Time <54 mg/dL was higher with full manual meal boluses (1.05 %) than with announcement only or no announcement (0.0 % each; P = 0.05)**.
- TIR 3.9-10 mmol/L did not differ: 83.3 % (boluses) vs 79.85 % (announcement) vs 81.03 % (none); mean glucose 6.65 / 7.34 / 7.05 mmol/L; insulin dose and carbs similar; no serious adverse events.
- Reading: in young people on oref/SMB, extra user-given insulin (full boluses) bought little TIR and was where the little level-2 hypoglycaemia occurred.

## Limitations

- Pilot, 16 participants, 3 days per arm, supervised camp; abstract-level; not powered for differences.
