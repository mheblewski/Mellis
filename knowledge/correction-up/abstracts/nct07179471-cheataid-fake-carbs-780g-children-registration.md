# "Fake Carbs" Practice in the Glycemic Outcomes in Children and Adolescents Using AID System (CHeatAID) - trial registration, no results [no results yet]

- **Authors:** Athens General Children's Hospital "P. & A. Kyriakou" (sponsor)
- **Journal/year:** ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07179471, first posted 2025-09-17 (status: recruiting at last update 2025-09)
- **DOI:** none (registration)
- **URL:** https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07179471
- **Licence:** paraphrased (public registry record)
- **Saved:** summary of the ClinicalTrials.gov record (API) on 2026-10-21 for the Mellis RAG corpus.

## What it is

The only study found that directly targets "fake carbs" (entering carbohydrates that were not eaten so the 780G gives a bolus to correct a high). It is a registered crossover trial; **no results have been published as of 2026-10-21**.

## Methods (planned)

- Prospective, open-label, randomised crossover; ~40 children and adolescents (7-18 y) on MiniMed 780G; periods with vs without the fake-carb practice, about 2 weeks each.
- Primary outcomes: change in TIR 70-180 mg/dL, time in tight range 70-140 mg/dL, time <54 mg/dL.

## Results relevant to correction boluses

- None yet. The registration states that "clinical evidence suggests this practice does not improve glycemic outcomes" but cites no study; this claim is **[unverified]** - no published study measuring fake-carb outcomes was found in this search.

## Limitations

- Registration only; estimated completion 2025-12 (status not updated since 2025-09).
