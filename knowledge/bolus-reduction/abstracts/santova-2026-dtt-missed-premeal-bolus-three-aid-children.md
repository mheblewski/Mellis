# Glycemic Outcomes Following a Missed Premeal Bolus: A Comparative Study of Three Automated Insulin Delivery Systems in Children with Type 1 Diabetes

- **Authors:** Santova A, Plachy L, Neuman V, Amaratunga SA, Slavenko MG, Obermannova B, Drnkova L, Pruhova S, Sumnik Z
- **Journal/year:** Diabetes Technology & Therapeutics 2026;28(8):828-836
- **DOI:** 10.1177/15209156251415187
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41645539/
- **Licence:** paraphrased (abstract only; full text not open access)
- **Saved:** summary from the PubMed/Europe PMC abstract on 2026-10-21 for the Mellis RAG corpus.

## Study

- **Design:** prospective interventional study (Prague paediatric centre).
- **Participants:** 43 children and adolescents with type 1 diabetes (mean age 13 years, mean HbA1c 6.5 % / 47 mmol/mol) using MiniMed 780G, Tandem Control-IQ or CamAPS FX (Ypsomed).
- **Challenge:** 30 g and 50 g carbohydrate as standardised enteral nutrition with the pre-meal bolus deliberately omitted; 4-h post-meal glucose AUC and CGM metrics compared between systems.

## Results (abstract)

- Mean glucose-rise AUC after 30 g: 780G lowest, then Control-IQ, then CamAPS FX (137 vs 144 vs 156 mmol/L·s·10^3, P < 0.01); similar order after 50 g.
- Time in level 2 hyperglycaemia (> 250 mg/dL) after 30 g / 50 g: CamAPS FX 26 % / 42 %, Control-IQ 9.9 % / 26 %, 780G 5.0 % / 21 % (P < 0.001).
- Authors' conclusion: AID systems differ in how well they compensate a missed pre-meal bolus; 780G and Control-IQ limited post-meal highs better than CamAPS FX, which may matter for children prone to omitting boluses.

## Notes for Mellis

- **Relevance to reducing a meal bolus when falling / predicted low:** indirect. It shows how much post-meal rise remains when a child eats 30-50 g with **no** bolus and relies on the algorithm - the worst case of "skipping" rather than reducing. Systems with automatic correction boluses (780G, Control-IQ; Trio with SMB is similar in spirit) compensated better than CamAPS FX (basal modulation only). Even with the best system, a 50 g unbolused meal gave about a fifth of 4 h above 250 mg/dL - supporting "reduce, don't skip".
- **Population:** children and adolescents (mean 13 years).
- **Evidence level:** non-randomised comparison between users of different systems; abstract only reviewed.
