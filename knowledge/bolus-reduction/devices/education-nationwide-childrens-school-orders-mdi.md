# School guidelines for a student with diabetes on basal/bolus insulin injection therapy (Nationwide Children's Hospital, Columbus, Ohio)

- **Authors:** Nationwide Children's Hospital Section of Endocrinology (listed physicians and APNs; interim director M. Kamboj)
- **Journal/year:** school order form/guideline, document 14923 (PDF metadata created 2017, modified 2026)
- **DOI:** none
- **URL:** https://www.nationwidechildrens.org/-/media/nch/specialties/diabetes-clinic/school-orders_basal-bolus-injections.pdf
- **Licence:** paraphrased

## Dosing

- Rapid-acting insulin within 15 min before eating; for an unpredictable eater, inject immediately after eating (eating limited to 30 min) if requested.
- Dose = carbs ÷ carb ratio + correction ((BG − target) ÷ correction factor). Example: 87 g at 1:12 = 7.3 U; BG 257, target 140, CF 50 → 2.3 U.
- No correction bolus if < 3 h since the last bolus, < 1 h since vigorous exercise, or a low occurred in the previous 3 h.

## Low at meal or snack time (key rule)

- BG < 70 mg/dL: 15 g fast carbohydrate (4 oz juice or 3–4 glucose tablets), re-test after 15 min, repeat until > 70 mg/dL.
- If the low happens at a meal or snack time: treat as above until BG is above 70 mg/dL, then give the usual meal insulin to cover the meal's carbohydrate.
- If the next meal is more than 1 h away, give another 15 g snack without insulin (carb + protein + fat).
- Exercise: BG < 100 mg/dL before activity → 15 g complex carb snack without insulin.

## Notes for Mellis

- **Relevance:** US pediatric protocol: treatment carbs separate and un-bolused; once > 70 mg/dL give the FULL usual meal dose (no reduction mentioned). Also: no correction insulin within 3 h after a low, which limits stacking after a hypo.
- **Population:** school-age children on MDI.
- **Evidence level:** hospital clinical protocol (expert opinion).
