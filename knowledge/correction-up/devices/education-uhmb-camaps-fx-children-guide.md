## Rules relevant to high glucose and corrections

- **Top tips:** bolus before eating; "Avoid manual corrections"; only use healthy sites; suspend the pump if disconnected; avoid over-treating hypos.
- **Unexpected highs:** if glucose is rising unexpectedly or not coming down promptly, "THINK CANNULA" - there is often nothing to see and no pump alarm even when the cannula has stopped working.
- **Hypos caused by stacking:** listed causes include late bolusing (the pump has already raised basal, so the total is too much) and giving a manual bolus on top of a rising basal rate - "try and be patient and avoid giving manual boluses".
- **Illness with higher insulin needs and NO ketones:** let the pump adjust; consider whether the cannula needs changing; if still high, use Boost; if that is not enough, leave auto mode and run manual mode with an increased temporary basal rate.
- **Illness with higher insulin needs AND ketones > 0.6 mmol/L:** come out of auto mode; give fast-acting insulin by pen per the usual sick-day rules; change cannula and insulin; add an increased temporary basal rate; restart auto mode once ketones have resolved and at least 2 hours have passed since the last pen injection (the algorithm does not know pen insulin was given).
- **Hypo treatment:** much less glucose than before is usually needed (start with half the usual treatment on first use of closed loop; a quarter if rebound highs occur with a sloping-down arrow).
- **Complex meals:** split boluses or the "slowly absorbed meal" option, or Boost, rather than manual extra insulin.

## Notes for Mellis

- **Relevance (high, paediatric):** a children's leaflet that tells families to avoid manual corrections on the loop, to treat a persistent high as a cannula problem first, and to use pen insulin only with ketones (> 0.6 mmol/L, the ISPAD threshold) after leaving auto mode, waiting at least 2 h after the last pen dose before restarting.
- **Population:** children and young people on CamAPS FX.
- **Evidence level:** [Ed] hospital paediatric patient information.
