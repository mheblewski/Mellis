# Auto-correction Boluses Contribute to Hypoglycemia Following Temporary Target Cessation During Hyperglycemia

- **Authors:** Welch A, Zulfiqar R, Black E, Falciglia M
- **Journal/year:** Journal of Diabetes Science and Technology 2025;19(4):1158-1159 (letter / case report)
- **DOI:** 10.1177/19322968251336187
- **URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12021852/
- **Licence:** paraphrased (publisher does not allow full-text reuse; no abstract)
- **Saved:** summary of the letter as displayed on PMC (read via a summarising fetch) on 2026-10-21 for the Mellis RAG corpus; numbers cross-checked in the fetched text only.

## What it is

Single case report (with a reply from Medtronic, Vigersky et al. 2025, not read) of recurrent evening hypoglycaemia caused by MiniMed 780G auto-correction boluses delivered when a long temporary target ended while glucose was high but already falling.

## Methods

- 21-year-old woman with type 1 diabetes, 780G, target 100 mg/dL, active insulin time 4 h, TDD 59.9 U; 14-day download.

## Results relevant to correction boluses

- Temporary target (150 mg/dL, blocks auto-corrections) used 3-8 h at work with post-meal glucose 250-400 mg/dL. While it ran, auto-basal went to its maximum, which **the authors say does not count toward insulin on board**.
- When the temporary target ended during hyperglycaemia the pump gave an auto-correction: 16 such boluses of 0.97-4.79 U (mean 2.53 U). **Three hypoglycaemia episodes (<70 mg/dL) within 3 h followed boluses of 2.44-2.94 U**; one illustrated case: 2.47 U delivered while glucose was already falling.
- The 780G's own safeguard did not reduce the bolus enough during a rapid fall. Fixes: limit temporary targets to exercise, target 120 mg/dL, AIT 5 h; post-temporary-target lows stopped at 4 months.
- Lesson for any correction on top of a loop: a correction computed from a high current value, while glucose is already falling under extra basal insulin that is not counted as active, can cause a low.

## Limitations

- One patient; mechanism inferred from downloads; 780G-specific (oref counts temporary basal above scheduled basal as insulin on board, so the IOB gap may not transfer directly to Trio).
