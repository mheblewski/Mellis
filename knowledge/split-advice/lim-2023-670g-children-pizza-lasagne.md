# Lim R. et al. (2023) – hybrid closed loop alone did not improve glucose after pizza or lasagne in children

Source: Lim R., Abraham M.B., Nicholls R., Fournier P., Hanbury A. Efficacy of the MiniMed 670G hybrid closed loop system in managing postprandial glucose excursion with high protein high fat foods in children and adolescents under free-living conditions. Journal of Pediatric Endocrinology & Diabetes 2023;3(2). doi:10.25259/JPED_18_2023.

- Design: free-living randomised crossover pilot; children and adolescents 8–18 years with MiniMed 670G; a standard high protein/high fat dinner (lasagne or pizza) twice a week for 4 weeks in manual mode and 4 weeks in auto mode; CGM for 7 h after each meal.
- Results (own words): 38 meals in 7 participants; the mean net incremental glucose area after the meal did not differ between auto and manual mode for either meal. Five of seven families felt more confident eating these meals in auto mode.
- Why it matters: an automated insulin delivery system does not by itself handle a high fat/high protein meal; the meal bolus strategy (timing and split) still matters. Small pilot; hypoglycaemia not reported in the abstract.
