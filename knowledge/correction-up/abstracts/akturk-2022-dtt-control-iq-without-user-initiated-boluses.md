# Efficacy and Safety of Tandem Control IQ Without User-Initiated Boluses in Adults with Uncontrolled Type 1 Diabetes

- **Authors:** Akturk HK, Snell-Bergeon J, Shah VN
- **Journal/year:** Diabetes Technology & Therapeutics 2022;24(10):779-783
- **DOI:** 10.1089/dia.2022.0162
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35714344/
- **Licence:** paraphrased (abstract only)
- **Saved:** summary from the PubMed/Europe PMC abstract on 2026-10-21 for the Mellis RAG corpus.

## What it is

Single-centre real-life study of adults with poorly controlled type 1 diabetes who gave almost no boluses on Control-IQ, compared with matched intermediate and high bolusers.

## Methods

- 30 adults; groups by share of insulin given automatically: >90 % automatic (minimal/no user boluses), 50-90 %, and 10-49 % (high bolusing); outcomes at 3, 6 and 12 months.

## Results relevant to correction boluses

- All three groups improved A1c and TIR; in the >90 %-automatic group A1c fell 1.6 +/- 0.8 % and TIR rose 19.3 +/- 6.4 points, **without more time below range**.
- Control-IQ's automatic 60 %-sized hourly corrections alone produced large gains in people who corrected nothing themselves.

## Limitations

- Very small (30), retrospective, matched groups; adults with high baseline A1c; abstract-level data.
