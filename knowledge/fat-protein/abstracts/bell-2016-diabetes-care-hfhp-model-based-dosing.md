# Optimized mealtime insulin dosing for fat and protein in type 1 diabetes: application of a model-based approach to derive insulin doses for open-loop diabetes management

- **Authors:** Bell KJ, Toschi E, Steil GM, Wolpert HA
- **Journal/year:** Diabetes Care 2016;39(9):1631-1634
- **DOI:** 10.2337/dc15-2855
- **PubMed:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27388474/
- **Licence:** not open access (or open-access status not confirmed). This file contains a paraphrased summary of the abstract and key numbers written for the Mellis corpus, not the publisher's text.

## Summary of the abstract (paraphrased)

- **Population:** 10 adults with T1D (pump users).
- **Design:** low-fat/low-protein (LFLP) vs high-fat/high-protein (HFHP) meal with the same carbohydrate and the same insulin dose; then the HFHP meal was repeated with an adaptive model-predictive bolus until target postprandial control was reached.
- **Result with same insulin:** HFHP more than doubled the glucose incremental AUC (13,320 ± 2,960 vs 27,092 ± 1,709 mg/dL·min; P = 0.0013).
- **Insulin needed for HFHP:** on average **+65% insulin (range +17% to +124%)**, delivered as a **30%/70% split over 2.4 h** (30% upfront, 70% extended).
- ISPAD 2022 (corpus file ispad/ispad-2022-nutrition.txt) adds that the individual optimal splits ranged from 10/90 to 50/50 and durations from 2 to 3 h in this study.

## Notes for Mellis

- Adults, open loop, lab conditions, very small n. Shows huge inter-individual range (17-124% extra). The "30/70" adult result contradicts the paediatric "60/40 or 70/30" result of Lopez 2017; see SUMMARY.md.
