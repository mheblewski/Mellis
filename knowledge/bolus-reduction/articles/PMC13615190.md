## Key points

- AID in young children: in a crossover trial (ages 1–7) closed loop gave 8.7 percentage points more TIR than sensor-augmented pump; under 6 years, AID is linked to about 3 extra hours in range per day [AID Systems].
- Activity/exercise modes cut basal delivery and work best when switched on **60–90 minutes** before activity [AID Systems].
- **5–7 g** of fast carbohydrate is usually suitable to treat a low in young children and avoid rebound [Hypoglycemia and Hyperglycemia Management].
- Pre-activity snacks when glucose is ≤120 mg/dL (6.7 mmol/L): 5–10 g complex carbs for light/short play, 10–15 g for moderate play (PE, recess); 15–20 g for long aerobic activity (e.g., swimming) if ≤140 mg/dL (7.8 mmol/L); amounts vary by child [Physical Activity].
- On AID, eating more carbs than needed to reach the algorithm's target can trigger extra insulin [Physical Activity].
- Check ketones when glucose stays above 250 mg/dL (13.9 mmol/L) for 2 h; blood ketones preferred in very young children [Hypoglycemia and Hyperglycemia Management].

## Meals and bolus timing at preschool

[Meal and Snack Time] Variable appetite leads to inaccurate and delayed boluses and more glucose swings. Staff may give pump or smart-pen boluses with caregiver authorisation per the diabetes medical management plan (DMMP); rapid carbs must always be available; staff and caregivers should share how much of the meal was eaten.

## Exercise on AID

[Physical Activity] For planned, post-meal or prolonged aerobic activity, lowering insulin 1–2 h before may prevent lows; spontaneous play may need complex carbs. Where a rise is expected (fasting morning activity, anaerobic or competitive exercise), usual insulin may continue. Monitor CGM before, during and 2–3 h after activity.

## Other points

Diluted insulin is risky and only for very low insulin needs under strict supervision [AID Systems]. Glucagon must be available; remote monitoring by staff is optional and needs a school-provided device [Hypoglycemia and Hyperglycemia Management].

## Limits of this summary

- Preschool children (about 2–5 years) in US daycare/school settings; excerpt only.
- Narrative expert review; carb amounts cite ISPAD 2022 and ADA material.
- No guidance on reducing a meal bolus for falling glucose.
- Read the original for clinical decisions.
