# Licences and attribution of the Mellis knowledge base

The knowledge base is information for people with type 1 diabetes and their caregivers. It is **not medical advice**
and does not replace the treating team. Summaries can contain mistakes; every document links to its original, which
is the reference for any clinical decision.

## Our own text

Summaries and notes marked `mode: SUMMARY_ONLY` are written by the Mellis project in its own words. They cite the
original work (authors, title, journal, DOI or URL) and section or page, and quote at most a few words. The facts and
numbers belong to the cited authors; the wording is ours and is distributed with Mellis under the Mellis licence
(GNU AGPL v3.0). The originals remain under their own copyright and are not redistributed.
