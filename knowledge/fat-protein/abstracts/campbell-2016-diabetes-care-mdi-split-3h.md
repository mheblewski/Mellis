# Carbohydrate counting at meal time followed by a small secondary postprandial bolus injection at 3 hours prevents late hyperglycemia, without hypoglycemia, after a high-carbohydrate, high-fat meal in type 1 diabetes (research letter)

- **Authors:** Campbell MD, Walker M, King D, Gonzalez JT, Allerton D, Stevenson EJ, Shaw JA, West DJ
- **Journal/year:** Diabetes Care 2016;39(9):e141-e142
- **DOI:** 10.2337/dc16-0709
- **PubMed:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27352952/ ; accepted manuscript: https://eprints.leedsbeckett.ac.uk/id/eprint/2801/
- **Licence:** published letter is not open access; the accepted manuscript is in the Leeds Beckett open-access repository without a stated open licence. Content below is paraphrased.

## Summary (paraphrased from the accepted manuscript; the letter has no abstract)

- **Population:** 10 men with T1D on MDI (insulin aspart + glargine n = 8 or detemir n = 2); age 26 ± 4 years; HbA1c 52.5 ± 5.9 mmol/mol (7.0 ± 0.5%). ClinicalTrials.gov NCT02595658.
- **Meals:** matched for carbohydrate and protein, different fat: low fat **68 g carbohydrate, 26 g protein, 5 g fat**; high fat **68 g carbohydrate, 26 g protein, 55 g fat**.
- **Four randomised conditions:**
  1. Low fat, 100% carbohydrate-counted dose.
  2. High fat, 100% dose.
  3. High fat, **130% dose given upfront**.
  4. High fat, 100% dose upfront **plus an extra 30% injected 3 h after the meal** (split).
- 6 h of venous glucose and insulin sampling.
- **Results:** high fat with 100% caused late hyperglycaemia. With **130% upfront, 60% of participants had hypoglycaemia (< 3.9 mmol/L)**; there was no hypoglycaemia in the other conditions. The **split (100% + 30% at 3 h)** gave a glucose profile similar to the low-fat meal, despite 50 g more fat.
- **Authors' message:** increasing the mealtime dose alone is not effective and can raise the risk of early postprandial hypoglycaemia; give the extra insulin about 3 h later.

## Notes for Mellis

- Strongest single piece of evidence that **extra insulin given upfront for a fatty, carbohydrate-rich meal causes early lows**, and that **delaying the extra part** avoids them. Adults on MDI, n = 10; not AID.
- Basis of ISPAD 2022's MDI sentence about 130% as a 100%:30% split at 3 h.
