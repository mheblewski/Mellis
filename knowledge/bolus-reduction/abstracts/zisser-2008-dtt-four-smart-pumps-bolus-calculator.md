# Bolus calculator: a review of four "smart" insulin pumps

- **Authors:** Zisser H, Robinson L, Bevier W, Dassau E, Ellingsen C, Doyle FJ, Jovanovic L (Sansum Diabetes Research Institute)
- **Journal/year:** Diabetes Technology & Therapeutics 2008;10(6):441–444
- **DOI:** 10.1089/dia.2007.0284
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19049372/
- **Licence:** paraphrased (subscription article; only the PubMed abstract was available)
- **Saved:** summary from the PubMed abstract on 2026-10-21 for the Mellis RAG corpus. Full text not read.

## Content (from the abstract)

- Review of how four "smart" pumps' bolus calculators compute a dose from current BG, target BG, carbohydrate, insulin sensitivity, insulin-to-carb ratio and duration of insulin action ("insulin on board").
- Main message: "Each pump company calculates insulin doses in a slightly different way." The article compares recommendations between the four pumps, lists errors that can occur when using calculators, and includes an in-silico simulation of a meal followed by a snack using several insulin decay curves.

## What could not be verified

- The abstract does not name the four pumps or give per-pump numbers, and does not state how each pump treats glucose below target (reverse correction) or whether IOB is subtracted from the meal part. Secondary web summaries were inconsistent (one mixed this paper up with Buchanan 2021), so no pump-specific claims are recorded here.

## Notes for Mellis

- **Relevance:** Early peer-reviewed confirmation that commercial calculators with the same settings give different doses, mainly through IOB handling; the detailed, verifiable comparison of below-target behaviour is Buchanan et al. 2021 (separate file).
- **Evidence level:** narrative review + simulation (abstract only).
