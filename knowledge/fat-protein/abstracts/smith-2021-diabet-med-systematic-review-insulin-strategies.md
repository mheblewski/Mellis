# Insulin strategies for dietary fat and protein in type 1 diabetes: a systematic review

- **Authors:** Smith TA, Marlow AA, King BR, Smart CE
- **Journal/year:** Diabetic Medicine 2021;38(11):e14641
- **DOI:** 10.1111/dme.14641
- **PubMed:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34251692/
- **Licence:** not open access (or open-access status not confirmed). This file contains a paraphrased summary of the abstract and key numbers written for the Mellis corpus, not the publisher's text.

## Summary of the abstract (paraphrased)

- **Design:** systematic review of RCTs, 1995-2021, reporting mean glucose, AUC, time in range or hypoglycaemia after fat or fat+protein meals. **18 studies** included.
- **Extra insulin:** 13 studies gave additional insulin.
  - Five studies gave **+30% to +43% of the ICR dose for 32-50 g fat**, and **+31% to +51% for 7-35 g fat with 12-27 g protein** added to control meals.
  - Eight studies used fat/protein algorithms giving **-28% to +75%** of the ICR dose for meals with 19-50 g carbohydrate, 2-79 g fat and 10-60 g protein; only one study showed benefit from giving less than an extra 24%.
- **Delivery pattern:** eight studies. In pump therapy, 4 of 6 studies found the **combination (dual-wave) bolus** superior; in MDI, 1 of 2 studies found a **split dose** superior.
- **Upfront amount:** five studies examined the split; four showed that **60% to 125% of the ICR dose upfront** was needed.
- **Timing:** two studies; both found that giving insulin **15 min before the meal** lowered postprandial glucose.
- **Conclusion:** benefit from **an additional 24% to 75% of the ICR dose** for fat and fat+protein meals; supportive evidence for a **combination bolus with at least 60% of the ICR dose upfront, 15 min before the meal**.

## Notes for Mellis

- This review is ISPAD 2022 reference 103 (the "25-75%, starting adjustment up to 60%, 15 min before, remainder over 3 h" sentence in the ISPAD nutrition chapter).
- Almost all included studies are open-loop (pump or MDI); none tested AID.
