# For a high fat, high protein breakfast, preprandial administration of 125% of the insulin dose improves postprandial glycaemic excursions in people with type 1 diabetes using multiple daily injections: a cross-over trial

- **Authors:** Smith TA, Smart CE, Howley PP, Lopez PE, King BR
- **Journal/year:** Diabetic Medicine 2021;38(7):e14512
- **DOI:** 10.1111/dme.14512
- **PubMed:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33421203/
- **Licence:** not open access (or open-access status not confirmed). This file contains a paraphrased summary of the abstract and key numbers written for the Mellis corpus, not the publisher's text.

## Summary of the abstract (paraphrased)

- **Population:** 24 children and adults on MDI (≥ 4 injections/day); age 9-35 years per Furthner 2021.
- **Meal:** same high-fat, high-protein breakfast (30 g carbohydrate, 40 g fat, 50 g protein) on 4 days.
- **Strategies:** 100% ICR aspart single dose (100Asp); 125% ICR aspart single dose (125Asp); 125% ICR regular insulin (125Reg); **125% ICR split as 100% before the meal + 25% 1 h after the meal (100:25Asp)**. Pre-meal doses given 15 min before eating. 5 h CGM.
- **5-h incremental AUC:** 100Asp 620 mmol/L·min [451, 788]; **125Asp 341 [169, 512]**; 125Reg 675 [504, 847]; 100:25Asp 434 [259, 608]. 125Asp significantly lower than 100Asp (p = 0.016) and 125Reg (p = 0.002).
- **Hypoglycaemia:** one episode, in the regular-insulin arm.
- **Conclusion:** giving 125% upfront with aspart improved glucose without hypoglycaemia; **no additional benefit from splitting (100:25 at 1 h)** or from regular insulin.

## Notes for Mellis

- Important counterpoint to "always split": for this meal (low carbohydrate 30 g, high fat + protein), adding 25% to the upfront injection worked and splitting at 1 h added nothing. Compare Campbell 2016 (abstracts/campbell-2016-diabetes-care-mdi-split-3h.md), where 130% upfront caused hypoglycaemia in 60% of adults after a high-carbohydrate (68 g), high-fat (55 g) meal. The difference in carbohydrate load and fat amount is a likely reason; see SUMMARY.md.
