# Feasibility Study of a Hybrid Closed-Loop System with Automated Insulin Correction Boluses

- **Authors:** Nimri R, Grosman B, Roy A, Nir J, Fisch Shvalb N, Kurtz N, Loewenthal N, Gillon-Keren M, Muller I, Atlas E, Phillip M
- **Journal/year:** Diabetes Technology & Therapeutics 2021;23(4):268-276
- **DOI:** 10.1089/dia.2020.0448
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33185480/
- **Licence:** paraphrased (abstract only)
- **Saved:** summary from the PubMed/Europe PMC abstract on 2026-10-21 for the Mellis RAG corpus.

## What it is

First home feasibility study of the Medtronic advanced hybrid closed loop (the algorithm later sold as 780G) with an optional automatic correction bolus mode, in adolescents and young adults, including missed and late meal-bolus challenges.

## Methods

- 12 participants (median 16.6 y); 6 days open loop with predictive low-glucose suspend, 6 days/5 nights supervised hotel stay with challenges (missed meal bolus, late meal bolus, exercise), then 3 weeks home use.

## Results relevant to correction boluses

- Time in auto mode 93.3 %; TIR 68.4 % (open loop) -> 74 % (closed loop at home); time <70 mg/dL 4 % -> 2.6 % (not significant); night TIR 64.6 % -> 80.7 % (P = 0.007) with no change in hypoglycaemia; A1C 7.1 % -> 6.8 %; no serious adverse events.
- Adding automatic corrections did not increase hypoglycaemia in this small adolescent group, including on challenge days.

## Limitations

- Single-arm, 12 participants, short; manufacturer co-authors; abstract-level (challenge-day details not in abstract).
