# Mitigation Strategies for a Missed Meal Bolus in People with Type 1 Diabetes Using the MiniMed 780G System

- **Authors:** Grassi BA, Onetto MT, Mora G, Tapia N, Passi Á, Jofré P, Castro M, Kurtz N, Vigersky R, Cohen O
- **Journal/year:** Diabetes Technology & Therapeutics 2026;28(4):374-380
- **DOI:** 10.1177/15209156251395010
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41264350/
- **Licence:** paraphrased (abstract only; full text not open access)
- **Saved:** summary from the PubMed/Europe PMC abstract on 2026-10-21 for the Mellis RAG corpus.

## Study

- **Design:** prospective, open-label, real-world crossover; remote supervision.
- **Participants:** 32 adults with type 1 diabetes using the MiniMed 780G (Chile; two authors appear to be Medtronic-affiliated - check disclosures).
- **Meals:** standardised, about 60 g carbohydrate, three strategies in each participant: (E1) normal pre-meal bolus; (E2) no bolus, leaving it to the system's auto-basal and auto-corrections; (E3) a late bolus covering **50 % of the carbohydrate, given 60 min after the meal**.
- **Primary outcome:** 4-h post-meal time in range 70-180 mg/dL.

## Results (abstract)

- TIR 4 h: pre-meal bolus 85.5 ± 18.8 %; late 50 % bolus 63.5 ± 24.0 %; no bolus 52.3 ± 25.3 % (pre-meal better than both, late half bolus better than none, P < 0.001). Time in tight range followed the same order.
- Time 54-70 mg/dL slightly higher with the pre-meal bolus but within consensus goals; no difference in time < 54 mg/dL between strategies; the late half bolus reduced time > 250 mg/dL versus no bolus; no severe hypoglycaemia or DKA.
- Authors' conclusion: pre-meal bolusing is best; if a bolus is missed, a correction of 50 % of the meal carbs 1 h after eating improves TIR without more hypoglycaemia; without it the 780G partly compensates.

## Notes for Mellis

- **Relevance to reducing a meal bolus when falling / predicted low:** indirect but numeric: a **half bolus given late** (after the system has already started reacting) did not increase hypoglycaemia on 780G. It matches the ATTD consensus rule (half the bolus 30-60 min after the meal start). It does not test a reduced bolus for a meal eaten while glucose is low or falling.
- **Population:** adults only; one system (780G, target and auto-correction logic differ from oref/Trio).
- **Evidence level:** small randomised-order crossover study in real-world conditions; abstract only reviewed.
- The 780G meal-management review already in the corpus (Edd et al. 2026, PMC12805926) cites an earlier report of this work (22 participants, TIR 87.2 % with pre-meal bolus) and recommends a 50 % bolus within 30 min after eating if the bolus was missed, and no manual bolus after 30 min.
