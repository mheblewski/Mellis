# Unexplained Hyperglycaemia and Sick Day Rules (Hybrid Closed Loop) - NHS Scotland flowchart (glucose > 14 mmol/L not responding to corrections, ketone-based pen doses of 10-20 % TDD, 4 h wait before closed loop)

- **Authors:** NHS Scotland (document author listed as Jennifer Thomas); published on My Diabetes My Way (NHS Scotland) and used by NHS Forth Valley
- **Journal/year:** NHS Scotland patient flowchart, version 2 (PDF dated 1 October 2025)
- **DOI:** none
- **URL:** https://mydiabetesmyway.scot.nhs.uk/media/3594/nhs-unexplained-hyperglycemia-and-sick-day-rules-for-cls-v2.pdf
- **Licence:** paraphrased (NHS Scotland material; short quotes only)
- **Saved:** summary from the PDF on 2026-10-21 for the Mellis RAG corpus. Education / clinical-guideline material (lower evidence than trials). The NHS Forth Valley page "Type 1 on insulin pump - hybrid closed loop" hosts the same flowchart.

## Rules

- **Trigger:** blood glucose above 14 mmol/L (about 250 mg/dL) and not responding to bolus corrections, or feeling unwell: check ketones.
- **Ketones below 1.5 mmol/L - minor illness, stay in closed loop:** change cannula, infusion set and reservoir; the closed loop "will try and cope" by raising basal and giving corrections; check sensor glucose against a fingerstick; take additional corrective doses via the bolus calculator if glucose is raised, even if not eating; usual bolus for carbohydrate; check glucose and ketones every 2-4 h; sip sugar-free fluids (at least 100 ml/h).
- **Ketones 1.5 mmol/L or above - severe illness, exit closed loop mode:** calculate average TDD; 1.5-3.0 mmol/L: 10 % of TDD of quick-acting insulin by pen or syringe; above 3 mmol/L: 20 % of TDD by pen or syringe; change cannula, set and reservoir; do not restart closed loop; use the usual carb ratio when eating; check glucose and ketones every 2 h.
- **Ongoing ketones (open loop):** 1.5-3 mmol/L: 10 % of TDD as a pump bolus every 2 h and basal +30 %; above 3 mmol/L: 20 % of TDD every 2 h and basal +50 % or more; usual meal bolus if eating (override the calculator); if not eating, about 20 g carbohydrate with bolus every 2-4 h.
- **Back to closed loop:** when ketones are below 1.5 mmol/L, wait 4 hours after the last pen/syringe injection of quick-acting insulin.
- **Seek help** after more than 4 hours without improvement; emergency hospital attendance if vomiting and unable to keep fluids down or unable to control glucose/ketones.

## Notes for Mellis

- **Relevance (high):** same structure as the ABCD-DTN UK leaflet but with a lower trigger (14 vs 15 mmol/L). Within closed loop and without significant ketones, extra corrections through the bolus calculator are explicitly allowed; outside that (ketones ≥ 1.5), the first doses go by pen and the loop stays off until 4 h after the last injection.
- **Population:** all ages (NHS Scotland HCL users); doses as % of TDD.
- **Evidence level:** [Ed] regional NHS patient guideline.
