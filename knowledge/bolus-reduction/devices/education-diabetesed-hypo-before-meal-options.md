# Rationale of the Week: Hypoglycemia Before Meal; Best Approach? (Diabetes Education Services)

- **Authors:** Diabetes Education Services (diabetesed.net, CDCES exam-preparation blog); no individual author shown
- **Journal/year:** educator blog post, about 2023 (undated; refers to an October 2023 conference)
- **DOI:** none
- **URL:** https://diabetesed.net/rationale-of-the-week-hypoglycemia-before-meal-best-approach/
- **Licence:** paraphrased
- **Saved:** summary from the web page on 2026-10-21 for the Mellis RAG corpus. Diabetes-educator teaching material (lower evidence).

## Case

- Adult with T1D, pre-lunch glucose 67 mg/dL, about to eat 45 g carbohydrate normally covered with 3 units (i.e. 1 U per 15 g).

## Options the educators accept as valid

1. Eat 15 g carbohydrate first to get above 70 mg/dL, then give the full 3 U and eat the meal ("rule of 15").
2. Reduce the bolus by about 1 unit (= the 15 g of the meal that act as hypo treatment are not covered), inject and eat immediately — described as a safe, standard intervention.
3. Eat the meal starting with the carbohydrate, and take the 3 U after finishing the meal.
- Answer key: all three are acceptable; choose with the person (person-centred), then review the results.
- Respondent poll: 36.7% chose "all of the above"; 20.4% option 1; 12.1% option 2; 10.2% option 3; 20.5% "only 1 or 2".

## Notes for Mellis

- **Relevance:** Direct educator endorsement of the Mellis concept: with a low at mealtime, part of the meal's carbohydrate (here 15 g = one ICR unit) can be treated as hypo treatment and left uncovered by insulin (bolus 3 U → 2 U), with no pre-bolus. Equivalent alternatives: separate 15 g treatment + full bolus, or full bolus after the meal.
- **Population:** adult T1D, MDI; not pediatric, not AID.
- **Evidence level:** educator opinion / exam rationale (no data).
