# A new proposal for a second insulin bolus to optimize postprandial glucose profile in adolescents with type 1 diabetes

- **Authors:** Marigliano M, Piona C, Tommaselli F, Maguolo A, Morandi A, Maffeis C
- **Journal/year:** Acta Diabetologica 2023;60(5):609-618
- **DOI:** 10.1007/s00592-022-02019-2
- **PubMed:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36705740/
- **Licence:** not open access (or open-access status not confirmed). This file contains a paraphrased summary of the abstract and key numbers written for the Mellis corpus, not the publisher's text.

## Summary of the abstract (paraphrased)

- **Population:** 15 adolescents with T1D on non-automated pumps with CGM (Verona, Italy).
- **Design:** standard, high-fat (HF) and high-protein (HP) real-life mixed meals, first with one pre-meal bolus. From the observed glucose differences the authors set a **second bolus 3 h after the meal of 30% of the pre-meal bolus for HF meals and 60% for HP meals**. The HF and HP meals were then repeated with pre-meal bolus + second bolus at 3 h.
- **Results with two boluses vs one:** glucose AUC reduced by **21% (HF) and 26% (HP)** (p < 0.05); time in range rose **from 52.5% to 78.3% (HF)** and **from 32.7% to 57.1% (HP)** (p < 0.01); mean glucose and time above range fell (p < 0.01); **no difference in time below range**.
- **Conclusion:** a second correction bolus 3 h after HF/HP meals is effective and safe.

## Notes for Mellis

- Meal compositions (per Marigliano 2026, articles/PMC13538753.md, same group): iso-caloric meals; HF meal 45.9% energy from fat, HP meal 24.4% from protein. Exact grams for the 2023 study were not read here.
- The same group later showed (2026, Control-IQ) that AID added +26% (HF) and +55% (HP) insulin by itself over 6 h but still did not normalise glucose, and suggested that a proactive manual bolus like this one is still needed on AID.
