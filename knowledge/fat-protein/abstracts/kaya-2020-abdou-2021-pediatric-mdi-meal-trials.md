# Two paediatric/adolescent MDI meal studies: fat-protein counting in adolescents (Kaya 2020) and high-protein vs high-fat breakfasts (Abdou 2021)

- **Authors:** Kaya N, Kurtoğlu S, Gökmen Özel H (2020); Abdou M, Hafez MH, Anwar GM, Fahmy WA, Abd Alfattah NM, Salem RI, Arafa N (2021)
- **Journal/year:** J Hum Nutr Diet 2020;33(3):396-403; Diabetes Metab Syndr 2021;15(1):7-12
- **DOI:** 10.1111/jhn.12711; 10.1016/j.dsx.2020.11.020
- **PubMed:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31647139/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33276255/
- **Licence:** not open access (or open-access status not confirmed). This file contains a paraphrased summary of the abstract and key numbers written for the Mellis corpus, not the publisher's text.

## Kaya et al. 2020 (paraphrased)

- 30 adolescents aged 16-18 years on basal-bolus (MDI); single centre, randomised crossover.
- Meals (Furthner 2021 table): standard 70 g carbohydrate/17 g fat/26 g protein; high-protein 70/26/36; high-protein-fat 70/30/36 dosed by carbohydrate only (HPFM-a) or by carbohydrate + fat-protein counting (HPFM-b).
- 0-90 min glucose did not differ; 120-240 min glucose was higher with HPFM-a than HPFM-b (P < 0.05). Late AUC (120-240 min) with HPFM-b 20,609 vs 24,092 (standard), 24,072 (HP), 25,986 (HPFM-a) mg/dL×min (P = 0.032).
- ISPAD 2022 cites this study as showing improved profiles without more hypoglycaemia (ICR from the 500 rule).

## Abdou et al. 2021 (paraphrased)

- 51 children and adolescents (Cairo), MDI, capillary glucose over 5 h after standard, high-fat and high-protein breakfasts (+125 kcal from protein or fat per Furthner 2021).
- High-protein: hyperglycaemia peaking at **3.5 h** and lasting to 5 h.
- High-fat: **early** hyperglycaemia peaking at **2 h**, then falling towards 5 h.

## Notes for Mellis

- Abdou's early high-fat peak **contradicts** most other studies, which show fat *lowers* the early (0-2 h) rise and raises the late one (Smart 2013, Bell 2020). Small fat increment, capillary sampling and a single centre limit it; treat as low-quality, but it is a reminder that individual responses vary.
