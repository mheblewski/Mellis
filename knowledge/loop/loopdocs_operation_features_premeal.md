# LoopDocs: Loop Pre-Meal Range

## The Loop Pre-Meal Range icon and what it does [section "Pre-Meal Range"]

The Loop pre-meal tool is the second toolbar icon from the left, a small clock between a knife and fork. It is grey until a Pre-Meal Range is set in Loop settings, then green and usable; while active its background turns green and a dark blue line on the glucose chart shows the pre-meal range.

It is a gentle way to get a little insulin working before a meal to limit the after-meal rise; LoopDocs says it is not meant to replace a normal pre-bolus. Example: with a usual target of 100-110 mg/dL and a pre-meal range of 80-80 mg/dL, Loop pushes toward the lower number, giving a kind of mini pre-bolus. Once tapped, Pre-Meal stays on for one hour, until carbs are entered, or until cancelled, whichever is first; starting an override also cancels it. Loop then adjusts the meal bolus for the extra insulin already given. Some people use it the other way round, to raise the correction range for an hour.

## Changing or removing the Loop Pre-Meal Range [section "How to Adjust Pre-Meal Range"]

Go to Loop Settings, Therapy Settings, Pre-Meal Range to change it. To remove the toolbar icon's function, tap Delete at the top right of that screen.

## Using Loop Pre-Meal safely [section "Assessing the impact of pre-meal"]

The Pre-Meal icon is Loop's "eating soon" mode. LoopDocs warns not to set pre-meal limits in a hypoglycaemic range that might need treatment. Deleting the setting disables the icon and prevents accidental activation. Some users make a custom override instead of using Pre-Meal: it can be started remotely through Nightscout, can have its own duration, and stays on after carbs are entered.
