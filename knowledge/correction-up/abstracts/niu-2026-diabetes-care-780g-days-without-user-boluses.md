# MiniMed 780G Advanced Hybrid Closed-Loop System Use During Days Without User-Initiated Boluses

- **Authors:** Niu F, Liu M, Yovanovich C, Vigersky RA, Shin JJ, McVean JJF
- **Journal/year:** Diabetes Care 2026;49(3):419-425
- **DOI:** 10.2337/dc25-2124
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41498529/
- **Licence:** paraphrased (abstract only; ADA licence forbids reproduction and text/data mining of the full text)
- **Saved:** summary from the PubMed/Europe PMC abstract on 2026-10-21 for the Mellis RAG corpus.

## What it is

Manufacturer real-world analysis of how the MiniMed 780G performs on days when the user gives no bolus at all, i.e. all correction is automatic.

## Methods

- Global CareLink data from 369,467 users (2020-2025); 54,553 had >=10 days without any user-initiated bolus; compared users with recommended settings (target 100 mg/dL, active insulin time 2 h) vs others; subgroups by age (<=15 / >15) and diabetes type.

## Results relevant to correction boluses

- On no-bolus days with recommended settings: mean sensor glucose 149.2 mg/dL, TIR 76.3 %, time <70 mg/dL 0.8 %, GMI 6.9 %; with other settings 159.7 mg/dL, TIR 69.3 %, TBR 0.9 %, GMI 7.1 %.
- So on days with no meal boluses and no manual corrections the system kept time below range under 1 %; aggressive settings let the loop correct more without more lows.

## Limitations

- Days without boluses are self-selected (may be days with little food); all authors Medtronic employees; abstract-level data; 780G only.
