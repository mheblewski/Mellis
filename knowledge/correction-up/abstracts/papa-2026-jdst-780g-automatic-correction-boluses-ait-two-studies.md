# Automatic Correction Boluses in the MiniMed 780G: Share of Insulin, Settings and Outcomes (two analyses of one Sicilian cohort)

- **Authors:** Papa G, Cannarella R, Finocchiaro C, Anzaldi M, Gusmano C, et al., Calogero AE
- **Journal/year:** Journal of Diabetes Science and Technology 2026 (online ahead of print): (a) "The Automatic Correction Boluses in an Advanced Hybrid Closed-Loop System: Algorithm Performance and Clinical Implications"; (b) "Effect of Active Insulin Time and Glycemic Targets on Automatic Correction Bolus Delivery"
- **DOI:** (a) 10.1177/19322968261446967; (b) 10.1177/19322968261465689
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42097861/ and https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42438920/
- **Licence:** paraphrased (abstracts only)
- **Saved:** summary from the PubMed/Europe PMC abstract on 2026-10-21 for the Mellis RAG corpus.

## What it is

Two retrospective analyses of the same 287 people with type 1 diabetes (adults and children, four Sicilian centres) using the MiniMed 780G for >=6 months with >=80 % auto mode.

## Methods

- Pump and CGM downloads; share of automatic correction boluses (ABs) in total daily dose (TDD) and in bolus insulin; regression and ROC analyses; comparison by active insulin time (AIT) and target (100-120 mg/dL).

## Results relevant to correction boluses

- ABs = **18.3 % of TDD and 33.1 % of all bolus insulin**; share higher in children and in shorter diabetes duration / shorter AID experience; AIT and carbohydrate intake independently predicted it.
- **Higher AB/TDD went with worse control** (lower TIR, higher time above range, variability and GMI); in children it also went with lower time below range. AB/TDD <=20.3 % predicted TIR >70 % (AUC 0.785); <=18.6 % predicted GMI <7 % (AUC 0.828).
- AIT 2 h gave more auto-correction insulin (as % of TDD and U/kg/day) and **better TIR**; targets between 100 and 120 mg/dL did not change AB delivery or TIR.
- Reading for Mellis: when a loop is already correcting a lot, the fix is meal dosing/settings, not extra manual corrections; letting the algorithm correct more (shorter AIT) helped without a reported hypoglycaemia signal.

## Limitations

- Retrospective, associational, single region; abstract-level data (hypoglycaemia details by AIT not reported in the abstract); 780G-specific.
